Call Check scam-call alerts (text messages)

  • Program: Call Check Scam-Call Alerts.
  • What you get: a text when someone you look after, who uses Call Check, receives a call that looks like a scam, so you can check on them.
  • How to join: text JOIN to (229) 410-3440 from your own phone, then reply Y to confirm. Joining is optional.
  • How often: message frequency varies. Texts are sent only when a suspicious call happens, usually a few per month at most.
  • Cost: Msg & data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP to (229) 410-3440 at any time. You will receive one final text confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further texts.
  • Help: reply HELP, or email thefocusfund@gmail.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information.

Messages you'll receive

  • You text JOIN. We reply: "Call Check: Reply Y to get a text if [Name] receives a call that looks like a scam. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
  • You reply Y. We reply: "Call Check: You're signed up. You'll get a text if [Name] receives a call that looks like a scam. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
  • You text HELP. We reply: "Call Check: Scam-call alerts for families. Help: thefocusfund@gmail.com. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."
  • You text STOP. We reply: "Call Check: You're unsubscribed and won't receive any more texts. Reply START to resubscribe."
  • An alert looks like: "Call Check: [Name] is on a call that may be a scam. Consider calling them now. Reply STOP to opt out."

[Name] is the name of the person you look after.

Using Call Check

Call Check is in early testing. Its warnings are guidance, not guarantees: it may miss a scam, and it may flag a call that is not a scam. Call Check does not replace emergency services; in an emergency, call 911.
