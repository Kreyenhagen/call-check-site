Call Check

Call Check protects older adults from phone scams. Calls to their iPhone are checked before they ring, and their family gets a text if a call looks like a scam.

How it works

  1. An adult child sets up Call Check on their parent's iPhone.
  2. Incoming calls are forwarded to Call Check, checked for signs of a scam, and then ring the phone as normal.
  3. If a call looks like a scam, the parent gets a notification on their phone, and family members who signed up get a text.
